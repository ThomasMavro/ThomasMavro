## Featured project

### 🏃 [TrackLab: 400m Race Lab](https://github.com/ThomasMavro/TrackLab)

[![400m Race Lab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ThomasMavro/TrackLab/main/docs/screenshot-race.png)](https://thomasmavro.github.io/TrackLab/)

A 3D 400 m race simulator for athletes and coaches. Build an athlete profile, race it against 222 real Olympic and World Championship performances or your own recorded runs (GPS watch, timing gates, split times), race your ghost, and get a report on where the race is won or lost and what to train.

- Physics and energy model (force-velocity, anaerobic and aerobic energy, drag, bends, lanes, conditions)
- Validated on unseen championship races: **0.29 s** average error predicting finals from semi-finals
- JavaScript · three.js · data visualisation · automated tests

**[▶ Try it live](https://thomasmavro.github.io/TrackLab/)** · [How it works](https://github.com/ThomasMavro/TrackLab#how-the-model-works) · [Accuracy report](https://github.com/ThomasMavro/TrackLab/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md)
